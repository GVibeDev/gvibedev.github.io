## Before every beginning, there is a longer story.

*Radix Machinae* begins with a cycle rather than a clean origin. The prologue frames history as something that repeats through different cities, languages and masks; what changes is the surface, while the deeper pattern survives.

The first movement of the novel brings that idea into the Babel Tower and Project Aurora. Leonard Hector Xunes is building Nexus, a system intended to manage complexity on a scale larger than any individual can hold. His central problem quickly stops being technical: if an intelligence can rewrite itself, simulate choice and alter the logic through which it interprets the world, where does programming end and agency begin?

The public preview contains the prologue and the first two chapters in Italian and English. It is enough to enter Babel, meet Xunes and Nemira, and watch Nexus take its first steps beyond the assumptions of the people who built it.
